# Rep5x - Ender 3 Pro assembly instructions

Printer-specific assembly steps for the Ender 3 Pro. Follow the main [assembly instructions](../../assembly-instructions-universal.md) and complete these Ender 3 Pro-specific modifications.

## Z-axis endstop relocation

The Z endstop needs to be moved from Z min to Z max position. The cable will need to be extended or replaced to reach the top of the printer.

1. Remove the Z endstop from its original position at the bottom of the Z axis
2. Relocate the endstop to the top of the Z axis (Z max position)
3. Extend or replace the endstop cable to reach the new position
4. Adjust the trigger position so the nozzle homes at the correct Z height

## Continue assembly

Follow main [assembly instructions](../../assembly-instructions-universal.md) parts 1-4 to complete the Rep5x conversion.
